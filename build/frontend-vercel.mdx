Follow [Ship on Vercel](/guides/widget/deploy/ship-on-vercel) to deploy a frontend
for your activated App. The current guide covers the compiled widget package,
registered host origins and explicit public provider configuration.

Your deployed App runs on Aomi; the frontend presents its conversation and wallet
interface. You can also use an activated App directly on `chat.aomi.dev` without
hosting a frontend.
