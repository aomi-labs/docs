Aomi ships payment rails so an App can be a revenue line, not just a
feature: you declare a price on a tool, users pay over HTTP, and settlement
lands onchain — directly in your wallet, without the money ever pooling at
Aomi.

The unit of account is the **credit**: 1 credit = $0.01.

## Pricing a tool

An App declares prices in a sidecar file, `<app>.pricing.toml`, next to its
manifest:

```toml
version = 1

[[beneficiaries]]
name  = "treasury"
type  = "evm_address"
chain = "eip155:8453"
value = "0xYourAddress"

[resources.get_idle_assets]
pricing     = { flat = 100 }   # 100 credits = $1.00 per successful call
beneficiary = "treasury"
```

Rules the runtime enforces: charging is **success-only** (a blocked or
failed call is never charged), unknown pricing keys fail the App load
rather than running it underpriced, and the beneficiary address is
snapshotted at load so accrued fees keep the address they accrued under.

## How a user pays: x402

Payment rides the HTTP layer via [x402](https://www.x402.org/). When a
turn needs payment, the backend answers `402 Payment Required` with a
signed challenge for the exact amount owed. The client — the portal, or
the CLI with `--payment-method coinbase` — signs a USDC
`transferWithAuthorization` (one wallet signature; the facilitator pays
the gas) and retries. The facilitator verifies and settles the transfer
onchain mid-request, and the transaction hash comes back as the receipt.

Two floors keep this from prompting on every message:

- **Platform usage** accrues against a running balance and is only
  challenged once you are ~$5 underwater.
- **Partner fees** — your priced tools — accrue per user in a bucket keyed
  to your beneficiary address. Once a user owes ~$1, their next message is
  answered with a 402 whose pay-to **is your address**. The user pays you
  directly, onchain; Aomi never holds the funds. The settlement is recorded
  with its transaction hash and shows up in your ledger with an explorer
  link.

## Watching the money: the ledger

The [Developer Platform](/build/platform) Usage page renders your
partner-payments ledger live from the statement API: per-tool prices,
observed calls, accrued vs settled vs outstanding, and an event trail of
`fee_accrued` → `settlement_confirmed` with explorer links. One receipt may
clear fees accrued across several of your Apps — settlement is per
beneficiary, not per tool.

Platform take rates are published in the builder statement: 10% on tool
fees, 30% on outcome fees, 10% on managed-model spend, $10/App/month
hosting.

## Status: live and rolling out

Honest limits, per house rule:

- **Live** — per-call x402 pricing and USDC settlement via facilitator;
  partner fee accrual and direct user→builder settlement with onchain
  receipts; the statement API and the Usage ledger; CLI payment
  (`--payment-method coinbase`). The full loop — priced tool, 402,
  signed retry, onchain receipt in the ledger — is proven end to end with
  Somm Finance's app on Base Sepolia.
- **Rolling out** — **MPP** (Machine Payments Protocol, Tempo's session-
  channel rail): deposit once into an escrow channel, sign cumulative
  off-chain vouchers, settle post-turn. The rail is implemented behind the
  same payment gate as x402 but is not yet active on the production path.
- **Rolling out** — **outcome fees**: `[[outcome]]` rules that add a
  bps-of-flow fee leg to the transaction batch itself, paid in the flowed
  token. Assembly and validation ship today; live broadcast is gated on
  the wallet-delegation setup described in
  [Signing policy](/concepts/signing-policy).
