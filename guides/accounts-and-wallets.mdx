---
title: "Sign in and manage wallets"
description: "Sign in, link another wallet, choose the active signer, and merge accounts when a sign-in method is already linked elsewhere."
---

Use one Aomi account for your chats, linked wallets, settings, and credits.
Connecting a wallet makes it available on this device. Linking it proves that
it belongs to your account. Choosing an **Active** wallet selects the address
that signs for its chain family on this device.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sign in">
    In the [portal](https://chat.aomi.dev), click **Sign in**. Choose **Privy**
    or **Para** for a provider login, or select a browser wallet under
    **Wallets**. If a wallet supports both Ethereum and Solana, choose the
    chain family you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the selected login">
    Privy or Para opens its own sign-in dialog. For a browser wallet, approve
    the connection and sign the ownership message. Aomi connects and signs you
    in through the same flow.

    Signing the ownership message does not send a transaction or transfer funds.
    If you dismiss the signature request, finish verification before using that
    address as a linked signer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your account and signer">
    Open **Settings → Account**. Under **Wallets & access**, the **signs with**
    strip shows one EVM slot and one Solana slot. Check the address you intend
    to use. Connection dots show whether a wallet is connected; **Active**
    identifies the signer selected on this device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The portal can recover cookie-owned guest chats and bring them into your
account when you sign in. A separate-origin widget does not merge guest chats
into an account. Its host can remember a selected thread identifier, but that
identifier does not grant access to a previous guest's conversations.

## Link another wallet or login

While signed in, open **Settings → Account → Wallets & access** and click
**Add a wallet**. Choose a browser wallet or a Privy/Para login and complete its
proof. A provider login is linked only after you explicitly choose it.

**Wallets** lists addresses that can sign you in with a message. **Social
sign-in** lists your linked Privy and Para logins with their wallets. Linking
does not enable automatic signing; configure that separately in
[**Settings → Safety**](/security/configure-approvals).

## Choose the active wallet

Open the EVM or Solana **signs with** slot and choose an address. You can also
switch wallets from the account menu. Each chain family has its own selected
signer, so choosing an EVM wallet does not replace the Solana selection.

An already linked, available address can become **Active** without another
ownership signature. If the address is not currently available, follow the
prompt to connect its wallet or switch to that address in the wallet app.

When your wallet app switches to an unlinked address, Aomi offers to verify it.
You can choose **Not now** and link it later. A connected address does not
become linked just because you selected it in the wallet app.

Finish or cancel a pending transaction review before switching its signer.

## Merge two accounts

If a wallet or provider login already belongs to another Aomi account, linking
it opens **Merge accounts**. The sheet shows the other account's chats, wallets,
and credits, along with any saved keys that will not carry over.

Choose **Merge into this account** to move those chats, wallets, and credits
into the account you are currently signed in to. The other account closes,
and its linked wallets and logins then sign you in to the remaining account.
The ownership or provider proof you just completed authorizes the offer; the
merge does not ask for a second ownership signature.

<Warning>
  The other account's saved model-provider keys and App secrets do not carry
  over. Review the sheet's list and configure the credentials you still need
  on the remaining account. Merging accounts does not move wallet funds or
  transfer private keys.
</Warning>

In the portal, **Switch to that account instead** lets you keep the accounts
separate and sign in to the other one. This option is not available in a hosted
widget; sign out and sign in to that account directly instead.

If the merge offer expires, repeat the proof to receive a new offer. If a reply
is still being billed, wait for it to finish and try the merge again.

## Rename, disconnect, or remove

Use each wallet's menu in **Wallets & access**:

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Rename** | Changes the address's display name in Aomi. It does not change the address or wallet app. |
| **Disconnect** | Ends the wallet connection on this device. The address remains linked to the account. |
| **Remove** | Removes the address from the account, so it no longer signs you in to that account. The wallet and its funds are unaffected. |

Removing a social login also removes its linked addresses, as described in the
confirmation. **Sign out** ends your Aomi session on this device; it is separate
from removing a wallet or deleting the account.

For signing modes, provider authorization, and revocation, see
[Configure approvals](/security/configure-approvals). For the identity and
permission model, see [Accounts and wallets](/concepts/accounts-and-wallets).
