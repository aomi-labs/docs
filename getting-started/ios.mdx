A native iOS experience is coming soon. You’ll be able to use Aomi Apps from your iPhone, review simulated transactions, and approve signing from your own wallet.

<Note>
  The iOS channel is not available yet. Use the [portal](https://chat.aomi.dev) in Safari or open [Telegram](/build/telegram) on your phone in the meantime.
</Note>

## What to expect

- A native conversation experience for Aomi Apps
- Mobile wallet connection and non-custodial signing
- Transaction simulation and approval from your phone
- Notifications for important transaction updates

We’ll publish installation and setup instructions here when the iOS channel is ready.
